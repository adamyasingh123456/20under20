SELECTION CRITERIA

What Does It Take to Make the List?

We’re looking for young people whose work shows achievement, impact, originality, initiative and the potential to go further.

01 • Achievement

Execution matters.

What have you actually done?

The concrete output, milestones achieved, and substance behind your work rather than aspirations alone.

02 • Impact

Show the difference you made.

Who or what changed because of your work?

The measurable reach, beneficiaries influenced, or problem genuinely solved across your community or audience.

03 • Originality

What you do with ideas.

What makes your work stand out?

Ideas matter, but so does what you do with them and how uniquely you approach the challenge at hand.

04 • Leadership

Initiative & action.

Did you take the first step?

Show initiative, ownership, grit, and the courage to begin without waiting for external permission or blueprints.

05 • Future Potential

Beyond where you are today.

What could this become?

We look beyond current milestones to the long-term trajectory and compounding potential of your ambition.

ABOUT THE ORGANISER

About Sunstone

Sunstone is a higher education company focused on making education more accountable to career outcomes. Through industry-aligned new-age programs, practical learning and career-focused experiences, Sunstone helps young people build the skills and capabilities needed to thrive in a changing world.

Why Sunstone 20 Under 20?

Exceptional work can begin well before conventional milestones. Sunstone 20 Under 20 extends the idea of recognising meaningful outcomes by celebrating what young people are already building, creating and changing, while bringing exceptional achievers together into a community that can grow beyond the Summit.
