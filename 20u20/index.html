Selection criteria

What Does It Take to Make the List?

We’re looking for young people whose work shows achievement, impact, originality, initiative and the potential to go further.

01 • Achievement

Execution matters.

What have you actually done?

The concrete output, milestones achieved, and substance behind your work rather than aspirations alone.

02 • Impact

Show the difference you made.

Who or what changed because of your work?

The measurable reach, beneficiaries influenced, or problem genuinely solved across your community or audience.

03 • Originality

What you do with ideas.

What makes your work stand out?

Ideas matter, but so does what you do with them and how uniquely you approach the challenge at hand.

04 • Leadership

Initiative and action.

Did you take the first step?

Show initiative, ownership, grit, and the courage to begin without waiting for external permission or blueprints.

05 • Future potential

Beyond where you are today.

What could this become?

We look beyond current milestones to the long-term trajectory and compounding potential of your ambition.

The core benchmark

“Evidence behind the story.”

Backing claims with links, repos, portfolios, certificates or verifiable documentation makes your profile stand out to our jury.

About Sunstone

Sunstone is a higher education company focused on making education more accountable to career outcomes. Through industry-aligned new-age programs, practical learning and career-focused experiences, Sunstone helps young people build the skills and capabilities needed to thrive in a changing world.

Who can be nominated?

Anyone who is under 20 years of age by the cutoff date (21 November 2026) and has a verifiable connection to Rajasthan (born, studied, living, or built significant work in the state) can be nominated across the five designated categories.

What qualifies as a Rajasthan connection?

Nominees may currently reside in Rajasthan, have completed key schooling/college in Rajasthan, have roots/family origin in Rajasthan, or have founded/directed projects delivering substantial direct value within Rajasthan.

Can I nominate myself?

Yes! In fact, self-nominations are strongly encouraged. You know your work, milestones, metrics, and documentation best. There is zero stigma attached to nominating yourself.

Can I nominate someone else?

Yes. Mentors, teachers, parents, peers, collaborators, and institutional leaders can nominate deserving candidates. The nominated individual will simply be invited to confirm details and submit supporting proof.

Is there an application fee?

No. Sunstone 20 Under 20 is completely free to enter. There are no submission fees, screening charges, or award fees at any point of the process.

What information is required?

Basic personal and demographic details, age verification, category selection, a concise summary of the achievement/work, and verifiable supporting links (e.g. GitHub repos, live sites, media features, pitch decks, award certificates).

How many achievers will be selected?

Exactly 20 honourees will make the final statewide cohort, divided evenly across the 5 primary categories (4 achievers per category).

What happens after a nomination is submitted?

Applications enter first-stage eligibility verification, followed by jury scoring and council deliberations from 22 to 30 November. Shortlisted candidates and final honourees will receive formal invitations to the Sunstone 20 Under 20 Summit in Jaipur.

What if the nominee is under 18?

A parent or legal guardian consent step will apply before the nominee’s personal information is processed through the relevant nomination workflow. Separate permissions may be required for event participation, photography, video recording and public publication of the nominee’s profile.

How will personal information be used?

All data collected will be handled strictly for evaluation purposes by the Sunstone 20 Under 20 council in accordance with privacy laws and will never be sold or shared with unsolicited third-party commercial entities.
